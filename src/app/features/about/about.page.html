Who We Are

Heavenly Corporation is a multi-service company built on one simple promise — where experience and expertise blend seamlessly with dedication and timelines. We operate across three core streams and are structured to take on more as we grow.

On the manpower side, we source, interview and supply skilled professionals across IT, hospitality, HR, masonry, plumbing and servicing roles. On the projects side, we take on transformer rewinding contracts and technical services — delivering them through a vetted network of specialists and verified vendors.

Whether you are hiring staff, awarding a contract, or requesting a one-off service, you work with a single accountable partner focused on quality and agreed timelines.
