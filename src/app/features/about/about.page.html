Who We Are

Heavenly Corporation is a leading manpower solutions provider dedicated to connecting skilled professionals with top employers across various industries. With years of expertise in the recruitment industry, we understand the unique challenges faced by both job seekers and employers.

Our comprehensive approach to manpower supply ensures that we deliver qualified candidates who not only meet the technical requirements but also align with the organizational culture and values of our client companies.

We specialize in multiple domains including IT, Hospitality, HR, Masonry, Plumbing, and Servicing Staff, providing tailored solutions that meet the specific needs of each industry sector.
